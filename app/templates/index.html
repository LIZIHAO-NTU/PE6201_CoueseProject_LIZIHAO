How it works
  1. Message analysisCombines a reviewed TF-IDF Logistic Regression model with auditable safety guardrails.
  2. URL inspectionChecks domain structure and brand impersonation without visiting the site.
  3. Evidence retrievalFinds related guidance from Singapore official sources.
  4. Optional LLM reviewThe language model reviews only a compact, redacted context when you turn it on.
  5. InterventionPrioritises actions based on what the recipient has already done.
